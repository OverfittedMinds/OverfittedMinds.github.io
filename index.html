Distributed Intelligence Lab · Gdańsk Tech

AI systems, distributed.

We study how models and software agents cooperate when data, computation, and responsibility are split between people, devices, and institutions.

Our work covers language models, multi-agent systems, and AI evaluation. We use privacy technologies and distributed protocols where they solve a concrete systems problem.

Research focus

A model is only one part of the system.

The systems we work on contain retrieval pipelines, tools, smaller models, people, and software that connects them. Some components run locally. Others belong to another organization.

That changes the engineering problem. Data cannot always be copied into one place. Agents may have different access rights. A useful answer may still need a trace showing how it was produced.

We study coordination, evaluation, and data protection at the level of the whole system. The result may be a benchmark, a protocol, a prototype, or a paper.

Current focusBehaviour of AI systems under constraints on data, trust, and computation.

Research areas

What we work on

Most projects sit between two or more of these areas. AI is the main subject; distributed protocols and privacy methods support it when centralization is not an option.

01 / Main areaAI

AI systems and evaluation

Language models, retrieval, domain models, and agent workflows. We test whether the assembled system is accurate, useful, and predictable on a defined task.

02 / SystemsDistributed

Agents and distributed execution

How agents divide work, exchange information, use tools, and reach a result when computation is spread across several environments.

03 / MethodsPrivacy

Privacy, verification, and coordination

Cryptographic and distributed mechanisms for projects that cannot rely on a central data store or a trusted operator.
