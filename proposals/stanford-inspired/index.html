About

We are a research group at Gdansk University of Technology working at the intersection of AI, distributed systems, privacy, and verifiable infrastructure.

Our focus is on systems that must operate under real constraints: sensitive data, adversarial environments, organizational boundaries, and deployment pressure. We care about AI that is not only capable, but measurable, safe, and accountable.

Location Gdansk, Poland
Department Computer Architecture, FETI
Group lead Prof. Julian Szymanski
Focus AI · Privacy · Distributed Systems

Research Areas

What we work on

01

Large Language Models & Applied AI

Evaluation, prototyping, and deployment of LLM-based systems for knowledge work, classification, decision support, and automation. We explore instruction tuning, retrieval-augmented generation, and efficient inference.

02

Trustworthy AI Systems

Reliability, robustness, accountability, and safety of AI systems used in sensitive organizational workflows. We develop methods for verification, monitoring, and alignment of deployed models.

03

Privacy-Preserving & Verifiable Computation

Systems that enable useful computation while reducing unnecessary data exposure and making critical claims verifiable. Topics include federated learning, secure multi-party computation, and zero-knowledge proofs.

04

HPC & Energy Aware Computing

High-performance computing infrastructure for AI workloads with a focus on energy-efficient computation, green AI, and sustainable data center design. Research into energy-aware scheduling, power-constrained optimization, and carbon-aware distributed systems.
